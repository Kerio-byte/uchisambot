These terms describe the rules for using UchiSamBot (Study Helper), a Telegram bot for school students. By using the bot, you agree to these terms.

## 1. The service

UchiSamBot helps students understand their homework: it gives step-by-step breakdowns, explains topics in simple language, checks solutions, and makes summaries and flashcards for revision. Subjects: mathematics, Russian language, physics, chemistry, history. The interface works in nine languages: Russian, English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Indonesian, Turkish, Vietnamese and Ukrainian.

## 2. How to use it

Open the bot at t.me/UchiSamBot, press Start, choose your language, and send a task as a text message or a photo. You can ask for a step-by-step breakdown, an explanation of a topic, a check of your own solution, or revision notes.

The bot is a learning aid: it helps you understand the method. How you apply the results — including at school — is your responsibility. We recommend using it to learn, not to copy.

## 3. Who can use it

The service is intended for school students and their parents. If you are a minor, please use the bot with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. You must also follow Telegram's terms of service.

The first 5 breakdowns are free for every user. After that, breakdown packs can be purchased with Telegram Stars: 1 breakdown — 30 Stars; a pack of 5 — 120 Stars; a pack of 15 — 300 Stars. There are no subscriptions and no auto-renewals. Payments are processed by Telegram — we never see your card or bank details.
